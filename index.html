Information

.About
un.moved studios is an award winning queer-led production house exploring human stories between image, culture, and lived experience. We are independent by nature and experimental by design with a focus on narrative storytelling. The approach is independent, research-driven and experimental: prioritising curiosity, collaboration and the freedom to make work that doesn't always fit neatly into a category. Our practice spans film, photography, writing and research. We believe a story can be a question, a witness, a small act of attention. It does not have to resolve itself neatly to remain true. We work with what is unstable, intimate, and still finding its form. The image is never neutral; it is always a way of looking, listening, and making room.

.Services
Research and Development / Writing and Editorial / Creative Direction / Film and Photography Production / Editing and Finishing / Distribution and Marketing.

.Ethos for the unresolved
We are not here to provide answers. We are here to ask better questions. To look closer. To sit with discomfort, contradiction and uncertainty. We believe in the independent spirit. In making things with what we have. In experimentation over perfection. In the imperfect, the intimate, the personal and the political. We know stories can resist. We make space for queer perspectives, alternative histories, cultural memory and voices that have been overlooked, misrepresented or never given the chance to be heard.

We remain curious. We remain independent. We remain un.moved
